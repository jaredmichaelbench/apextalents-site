Respec

Talent planner for World of Warcraft: Forever, for iPhone.

Support

Questions, bugs, or wrong talent data: email jaredwowbench@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, the iOS version, and the talent-data build shown at the top of the planner.

How talent data updates

Talent data ships inside the app and can update on its own when the game changes, without an App Store release. The planner tells you when newer data is ready.

Privacy

Respec has no accounts and collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
