Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Respec does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app stores on your device

Your talent plans and the last class you opened are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network use

The app downloads updated talent data from a public file server. That request carries no account, identifier, or personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
